## AI Hypercomputer

AI Hypercomputer is a supercomputing system that is optimized to support your artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) workloads. It's an integrated system of performance-optimized hardware, open software, ML frameworks, and flexible consumption models.

---

## System-Based Architecture

The core idea of this architecture is that it is designed and optimized as a complete, integrated whole, rather than just assembling individual high-performance parts. Google isn't just offering components in isolation, but a system where they are tuned to collaborate. Key components include:

- **Hardware**: Fast TPUs, GPUs, and fast networking specifically chosen and interconnected.

- **Software**: Open software and ML frameworks to support demanding tasks.

- **Consumption Models**: Flexible options integrated into the system structure.

This approach ensures that all "Lego blocks" of the system collaborate optimally for the most demanding AI and machine learning tasks.
