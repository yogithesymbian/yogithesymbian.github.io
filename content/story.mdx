### english

I’ve been in a phase where my main focus was solely on tasks and deadlines. However, after experiencing various team dynamics, I realized that the success of a project is also greatly influenced by the emotional well-being of the team members.

I’ve been through that phase as well, and since then, I’ve consciously chosen to be more attuned to the individual needs of the team. When someone seems burnt out, I try to provide the right support — whether it's re-scheduling, actively listening, or simply giving them space to breathe.

Just like AI with theory of mind that understands emotional context, I intentionally choose to offer more personalized solutions — only to those situations or individuals who truly need it, flexibly and contextually. By choice, not by chance.
