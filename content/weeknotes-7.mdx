## Performance Optimized Infrastructure

To show how the AI Hypercomputer comes to life in a data center, the process begins with setting up a training job for machine learning. The core of this system is its performance-optimized infrastructure. A scheduler or orchestrator kicks off a training job, which creates a compute cluster tailored for the workload.

---

## Accelerators: GPUs and IPUs

For AI workloads, instances utilize specific accelerators:

- **Nvidia GPUs**: Computer racks are filled with A3 VMs powered by Nvidia's H100 Tensor Core GPUs.
- **IPUs (Intelligence Processing Units)**: Custom-designed 200 GB/s IPUs create a separate data channel allowing GPUs to communicate directly with each other, effectively bypassing the CPU host.

---

## Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)

TPUs are custom-built in-house and specifically designed for artificial intelligence. Key features include:

- **Architecture**: Each chip features tensor cores with four Matrix Multiplication Units (MXUs) for arithmetic operations.
- **Scalability**: On a single machine, TPUs can have 1, 4, or 8 chips. For multi-host workloads, they scale to hundreds or thousands as a "pod" or "pod slice."
- **Massive Scale**: Multi-slice training jobs can utilize tens of thousands of chips simultaneously.

---

## Sustainability and Efficiency

The move to OCS (Optical Circuit Switching) for networking has created the opportunity to reduce power consumption by up to **40%**. This high-performance, energy-efficient, and power-dense infrastructure offers a tangible path to more sustainable IT.
